About MorphoPack

🌱 Mission

MorphoPack uses AI + Blender 4.5 to convert 3D product meshes into sustainable, manufacturing-ready packaging dielines — reducing material waste and CO₂ emissions.

⚙️ Pipeline

Upload STL/OBJ → Blender processes: clearance displacement, WELD, DECIMATE, CLEANUP → exports GLB (3D preview) + SVG dieline → sustainability metrics computed in real-time.

📦 Materials Supported
🌱 Agricultural Mycelium 📦 Corrugated Cardboard ♻️ Recycled Kraft
